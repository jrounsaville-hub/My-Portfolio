About Me

I'm a software engineer in training at Base Camp Coding Academy in Water Valley, Mississippi. I currently know Python, HTML, CSS, and SQL, and over the next year I'm expanding that into a full-stack skill set including Java, JavaScript, Django, React, and Spring Boot, while collaborating closely with peers on team projects. That teamwork is sharpening the communication and collaboration skills I'll carry into my career.

What draws me most to this field is problem-solving. I love the process of tracking down a bug and figuring out exactly what's causing it. I haven't had professional experience yet, but I've built a solid technical foundation and I'm ready to put it to work. Right now I'm especially interested in cybersecurity and backend development, and I'm using this year to explore both before committing to a specific path.
